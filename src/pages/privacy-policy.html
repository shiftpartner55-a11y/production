Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{build.date}}

This Privacy Policy explains how Shiftingpartner ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit {{site.url}} or contact us. We process personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information we collect

  • Consultation requests: full name, phone number, email address, pickup and drop locations, move type and lift availability.
  • Contact enquiries: full name, email address, phone number, subject and message.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the website secure and working well. If analytics tools are added, they will be listed here.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a relocation quotation.
  • To contact you by phone, email or WhatsApp about your requested move.
  • To plan and coordinate services you choose to book with us.
  • To prevent spam, abuse and fraudulent submissions.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your details for unrelated marketing without your consent.

3. Where your information is stored

Form submissions are transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in a secured Google Workspace spreadsheet accessible only to authorised Shiftingpartner staff. Our blog content is hosted using Supabase. Our service providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

4. Retention

We keep enquiry and consultation details only for as long as needed to respond, deliver services, meet legal or accounting obligations and resolve disputes, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing {{site.email}}. We will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Cookies

Our website uses only essential browser storage to make forms work (for example, keeping your entries if a submission fails). Embedded services such as Google Maps or WhatsApp may set their own cookies when you use them.

7. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party websites such as WhatsApp, Google Maps and social media platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, restricted access and spam protection. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

10. Contact / Grievance officer
