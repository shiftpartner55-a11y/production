Frequently asked questions

Everything You Need to Know About Your Move

Planning a house shift can bring up many questions. Here are clear answers to the most common ones.

Get Free Consultation Ask a Question
What services does Shiftingpartner offer?

Shiftingpartner provides household relocation solutions, including local house shifting, intercity relocation, packing, loading and unloading coordination, and transportation arrangements.

Do you provide local and intercity house shifting?

Yes. We provide local household shifting within our service locations and assist with relocation between supported cities. Share your pickup and delivery addresses to confirm availability and whether your route is serviceable.

Which cities does Shiftingpartner serve?

Our current service cities are Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Delhi, Mumbai and Coimbatore. Service availability depends on the pickup and delivery locations.

How can I get a quotation for my house shifting?

Contact our team with your pickup address, delivery address, preferred moving date and approximate household goods details. Based on your requirements, we will provide a quotation. The quickest way is our free consultation form.

How is the house shifting cost calculated?

The cost of relocation may depend on several factors:

  • Distance between pickup and delivery locations.
  • Quantity and volume of household goods.
  • Type of packing materials required.
  • Floor level and lift availability.
  • Loading and unloading requirements.
  • Additional services requested.

Our team will discuss the applicable charges before confirming your booking.

Do you provide packing materials?

Yes, we provide packing materials suited to your household belongings.

How do you handle fragile household items?

Fragile belongings such as glassware, crockery, mirrors and electronic items require careful packing and handling. Please inform our team about delicate or special items while requesting a quotation so that suitable arrangements can be discussed.

How early should I book my house shifting?

We recommend contacting us in advance, particularly during weekends, month-end periods and peak moving seasons. Early communication helps us check availability and plan your relocation.

Can I shift only a few household items?

Yes. You can share the details of the items you need to move. Our team will review your requirement and confirm the options available.

Can I change my moving date after booking?

Please contact our team as early as possible if you need to change your moving date. Any changes will depend on availability and the applicable booking terms.

How can I book Shiftingpartner's services?
  1. Contact our team with your moving requirements.
  2. Share your pickup and delivery locations.
  3. Provide details of your household belongings.
  4. Review the quotation and service inclusions.
  5. Confirm your booking and moving date.
