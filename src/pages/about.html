Our story

Built Around What Families Need on Moving Day

Every home holds belongings that matter — furniture chosen over years, kitchenware used every day, and items that carry memories. Shiftingpartner was created to give households one dependable partner for the entire relocation, instead of coordinating packing, labour and transport separately.

We started with a simple idea: a successful move begins with proper planning and careful execution. That is why we take time to understand your requirements before your move, explain the quotation clearly and keep you informed throughout the agreed process.

Today we support household customers with local and intercity relocation across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi and Coimbatore — and we continue to improve with every move.
