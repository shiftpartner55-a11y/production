Moving in Mumbai

What to plan for in Mumbai

Local factors that shape how we plan and pack your Mumbai move.

Society NOC and timings

Most housing societies require an NOC and allow moves only at certain times. Service lifts may need advance booking. We schedule your move accordingly.

Monsoon-ready packing

Mumbai's monsoon (roughly June to September) brings heavy rain. Waterproof wrapping and careful scheduling help keep your belongings dry.

Compact spaces

Narrow staircases and small lifts can require furniture to be dismantled. We plan dismantling and reassembly needs during your consultation.

Mumbai FAQs

Questions about moving in Mumbai

More answers on our main FAQ, or contact our team.

Do you handle moves within Thane and Navi Mumbai too?

We support household shifting across serviceable areas of the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, including Thane and Navi Mumbai. Share your addresses to confirm availability.

Who arranges the society NOC?

The NOC is usually issued by your housing society to the resident. We will tell you what details are typically needed and plan the move within the permitted timings.

Do you offer Mumbai to Pune house shifting?

Yes. Mumbai to Pune is one of the routes we support, along with Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi, Chennai and Coimbatore, subject to confirmation.
